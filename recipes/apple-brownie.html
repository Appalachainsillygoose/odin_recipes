Apple Brownies

Description

A warm, gooey, bite of fall as cinnamon apple meets moist blondie.
Imagine a softer coffee cake with bits of tart apple.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted
  • 1 egg
  • 3 medium apples - peeled, cored, and thinly sliced
  • 1 cup AP flour
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F. Grease a 9x9 inch baking dish
  2. Beat the sugar, melted butter, and egg in a large bowl until fluffy.
    Fold in the apples.
  3. Sift the remaining ingredients together in a seperate bowl.
  4. Slowly add the dry ingredients into the apple mixture until just blended.
  5. Spread batter evenly into the prepared baking dish, bake in oven for approx.
    35 minutes or until it passes the toothpick test.
