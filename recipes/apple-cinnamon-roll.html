Apple Cinnamon Rolls

Description

A different take on the classic cinnamon roll.
An apple filling and maple cream cheese
frosting make this a perfect fall bake.

Ingredients

For the dough

  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 2 1/4 teaspoons active dry yeast
  • 1 egg, room temperature
  • 1 egg yolk, room temperature
  • 3 1/4 cups AP flour
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, room temperature
  • Cooking spray or oil to grease the bowl

For the filling

  • 2/3 cup dark brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon cinnamon
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 2 medium sized apples, peeled and finely chopped

For the frosting

  • 4 ounces cream cheese, room temperature
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter, room temperature
  • 2 tablespoons maple syrup
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 3/4 cup powdered sugar

Steps

  1. Warm the milk. Add the milk, sugar and yeast to bowl of a stand mixer.
    Let stand for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the egg and yolk and mix until combined using a dough hook.
  3. Add the flour and salt and continue to mix on medium until dough forms.
  4. Slowly mix in the butter, waiting for it to incorporate.
  5. After all the butter has incorporated, knead the dough on medium speed
    for 8 minutes
  6. Transfer dough to a greased bowl and let rise for 1-2 hours
  7. On a floured surface, roll out the dough flat.
  8. Mix the brown sugar and cinnamon together in a bowl
  9. Spread the softened butter over the rectangle of dough
  10. Sprinkle the cinnamon sugar over the dough and top with the apple pieces
  11. Tightly roll the dough into a log and cut into 1-inch pieces
  12. Place the pieces in a baking dish, cover, and let sit for 30 minutes
  13. As the rolls are resting, preheat the oven to 350 degrees F
  14. Bake for 25 minutes, until golden brown
  15. As the cinnamon rolls are baking add the cream cheese and butter for the frosting
    to a bowl and using a hand mixer, beat them together.
  16. Add the maple syrup and vanilla and mix until incorporated
  17. Add the powdered sugar 1/4 cup at a time, mixing until all sugar is incorporated
  18. After the cinnamon rolls come out of the oven, let them cool for 10 minutes then
    spread the frosting over top of them and serve warm.
