Butter Swim Biscuits

Description

A savory, buttery biscuit with a soft, fluffy inside. Perfect for breakfast sandwiches.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter
  • 2 1/2 cups AP flour
  • 4 teaspoons baking powder
  • 4 teaspoons white sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 3/4 cups buttermilk

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees F
  2. As the oven is preheating, place your butter in am 8x8 baking dish and place it in the oven to melt.
    Be careful not to leave it in too long, just long enough to fully melt.
  3. Sift the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt together in a bowl
  4. Stir in the buttermilk until combined
  5. Taking your dough out of the bowl, carefully place it in the baking dish over the melted butter.
    Using a baking spatula, spread the dough to fill the corners of the baking dish.
  6. Using the same spatula, cut your dough into nine squares.
  7. Place the dough in the oven and bake for approximately 20-25 minutes, or until brown on top
